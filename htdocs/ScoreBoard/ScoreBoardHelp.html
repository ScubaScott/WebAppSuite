ScoreBoard Help

Instructions for using the timer, scoring, and history features.

Match Lifecycle & Control Bar

  • Idle: Displays [New Game]. Board controls (scores, fouls, period) are safely locked until a game is configured.
  • Ready: Displays [Start] and [End / OT]. The clock is primed for the upcoming period.
  • Running: Displays [Pause] alongside [End Period] (or [End / OT] if in the final period or overtime). Scoring and penalty cards are active only while the clock is running.
  • Paused: Displays [Resume] alongside [End Period] or [End / OT]. Scoring and cards are locked while the clock is paused.
  • Final: Displays [New Game]. The board is locked to preserve the completed match result.

Dual Clocks & Overage

  • Period Countdown (Main): Shows remaining time in the current period (e.g. 40:00.0). The clock is display-only (clicking it does not open the time roller). When the countdown reaches zero, the whistle sounds and the clock transitions into overage (e.g. -01:23.4) with an amber/red overage badge.
  • Period Elapsed: Readout beneath the countdown tracking continuous playing time elapsed within the active period. Tap it while the clock is running or paused to open quick minute corrections (+5, +1, -1, -5 minutes) if the clock was started late or early.
  • Clock corrections: A correction moves the start of the period, not just the display. The Start row in the history shows the corrected time (marked with *), a Clock +1:00 row records the correction, and goals and cards logged in that period move with it. Pauses keep their length. Remove the Clock row to undo the correction. A correction that would put an entry before the start of the period, or past the end of it, is refused with a message. Goals recorded by older versions of the app keep their original times.

Match Setup & Period Settings

  • Tap New Game or the card menu button (⋮ in the top-right corner) at any time to open Match Setup.
  • Configure Our Team and Opponent (name and color), a Theme, total regulation periods (1–8 periods), and duration presets (12m, 20m, 35m, 40m).
  • Our Team is always the team you are scoring for and appears on the left. When you are signed in, the names of your earlier teams are suggested as you type.
  • The Theme is saved with the game when you press Start, so the console, spectators and anyone who opens the game later all see the same look. You can change it until the first Start; after that it is fixed.
  • The interactive dual-wheel time roller is accessible directly within this modal (tap Custom Duration (Roller)) to dial custom minutes and seconds.
  • Tapping the Period indicator while stopped or paused also opens the match setup editor.

Overtime & Ending Matches

  • When in the final regulation period or an extra period, tapping End / OT opens options to:
    • Finalize Match (Final): Concludes the match, marks it final in active game feeds, and locks the scoreboard.
    • Add Overtime / Extra Period: Configures an overtime period (e.g. OT, OT2) with custom duration while preserving all accumulated scores.

Background Drift Recovery

  • If the device goes to sleep or the browser tab is minimized while running past the scheduled period duration, the app detects the drift upon return and prompts the operator:
    • End Period on Time: Caps the period at the scheduled duration (e.g. 40:00.0) and transitions to the next period.
    • Continue in Overage: Keeps the accumulated extra time and lets the clock continue running in overage.

Score Tracking & History Log

  • Tap a team's score area to increment that team's score.
  • Each score entry is logged in the Score History with Team (Score), custom note, period tag (e.g. P1, OT), and period elapsed time. Pause, resume and end rows list their position in the period; the period start row lists the time of day.
  • Tap any history row to open its options menu:
    • Add Note / Edit Note: Attach a custom single-line note (up to 60 characters) to that event.
    • Share: Opens the native share sheet or copies event text to clipboard.
    • Remove: Deletes the selected entry and decrements the associated score.

Period Rollback Fail-Safe

  • If a period was ended by accident, find the End of P{n} entry in the Score History and choose Remove.
  • The app will automatically roll back the match to the previous period, restoring its exact elapsed time and paused state so play can continue without losing data.

Penalty Cards (Fouls)

  • Enable Show Penalty Cards in Settings to display Yellow and Red card buttons below each team's score.
  • Tapping a card button while the clock is running logs the foul to the score history (team, card degree, and elapsed game time) and plays a card-raising animation. Cards are disabled while the clock is stopped.
  • Foul card entries can be tapped in the history list to add notes, share the card event, or remove accidental entries without altering scores.

Audio & Mute

  • A whistle sound plays when a period reaches zero.
  • Use the Mute button (lower-right) to toggle sound on/off; this setting is saved across reloads and resets.
